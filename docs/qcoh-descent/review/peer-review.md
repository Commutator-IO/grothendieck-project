# Peer review (working draft): « Descent of quasi-coherent sheaves along universal effective epimorphisms of schemes »

Working draft, produced with the local `peer-review` skill on the author's own manuscript (authorised by the author, local only; intake validated `READY_FOR_LOCAL_REVIEW`, review id QCOH-DESCENT-SELF). It is not an independent referee report: the reviewer is the drafting assistant, and the competence limit declared at intake is set-theoretic topology (the facts about $\omega^*$ are taken from the cited sources, whose proofs were read but not re-derived from scratch). No reporting guideline applies (pure mathematics, no data or statistics); the guideline selector, the statistics audit and the citation script for Pandoc citations were not used.

## Scope and map

- Question: does pulling back quasi-coherent sheaves along a universal effective epimorphism of schemes give full faithfulness (descent) or an equivalence (effective descent)?
- Claims: (A) full faithfulness for every universal effective epimorphism, no quasi-compactness; (B) effectivity for quasi-compact ones (credited to Mesablishvili, independent proof); (C) under « some nonprincipal ultrafilter on the primes is generated by aleph_1 sets », failure of effectivity along the Stacks 0EUG covering, so QCoh is not a stack for the canonical topology, on schemes and on affine schemes; a criterion (Prop. 5.4) for effectivity along that covering; the ZFC case left open.
- Evidence: complete proofs in the text; Stacks Project tags, Mesablishvili, Olivier, André–Fiorot for the affine case; Fine–Gillman, Pitz–Suabedissen, van Douwen–Kunen–van Mill, Pearl for the topology of $\omega^*$; a Lean file for the affine core of (A).

## Strengths

- The three answers are separated cleanly and each is credited: what is classical (affine case), what is Mesablishvili's (B), what was not found in print (A without quasi-compactness, C, Section 5).
- The proofs are complete and short; the reduction of effectivity along $\mathcal W_U$ to a combinatorial condition (Prop. 5.4) makes the role of set theory explicit.
- The consequence for the affine canonical topology (the remark on André–Fiorot, Thm 3.2, for infinite families) is stated with its exact scope.

## Major comments

1. **Location:** §1, definition of the canonical topology. **Observation:** the statement that a single-arrow sieve covers iff the arrow is a universal effective epimorphism is cited to Stacks 00WP and 00ZA, which are definitions only. **Why it matters:** this fact is used in the transitivity step of Theorem 4.1. **Action:** add a reference that states it (André–Fiorot §1.2, after Prop. 1.3, which recalls SGA 4, IV 4.3.9). *Fixed.*
2. **Location:** Theorem 4.1, Step 5. **Observation:** « an open and closed part of the immersion » is not meaningful; what is used is that $U\times_XU\to U\times_YU$ is an immersion, the base change of the diagonal, along which the compatibility of $\psi$ can be restricted. **Action:** reword. *Fixed.*
3. **Location:** Lemma 5.5. **Observation:** a clopen set of $O$ determines each $g_A$ only up to a finite set, so the correspondence is between clopens and coherent families up to finite changes of each member; triviality is invariant under such changes. **Action:** say so. *Fixed.*
4. **Location:** Remark 4.2. **Observation:** in the Buffalo appendix the remark had no argument. **Action:** give the argument (descent on $X_0$ by Theorem 4.1, then descent of the isomorphism along $X\times_YX_0\to X$ by Theorem 3.1). *Done in the draft; checked.*

## Minor comments
