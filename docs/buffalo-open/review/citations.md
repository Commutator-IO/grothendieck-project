## Specific questions

- **Johnstone–Joyal and Sh([0,1], Scott).** We could not open JJ 1982 or Johnstone, "Injective toposes" (LNM 871, 1981). We found no statement that this topos is quasi-injective. The fact now rests on a short argument in the article: the Scott frame is a retract, in frames, of the frame of upper sets of [0,1], so the topos is a retract of the presheaf topos [[0,1], Set]. That is the definition of quasi-injective. The retraction was checked by hand (finite meets, joins, composite equal to the identity). "Probably false" was therefore changed to "False" (abstract, status, table, closing paragraph), with the argument marked (e). Michel should confirm that he accepts this step without a printed source.
- **Lucyshyn-Wright numbers**: 1.5.4 and 1.5.9 are correct (arXiv v3).
- **Gabriel–Ulmer Satz 7.13; Adámek–Rosický 1.46, 2.78**: these agree with secondary sources (nLab), but the books were not opened. **AR 1.39** could not be confirmed and was removed. **AR ch. 6** (VP case) was not opened.
- **Makkai–Paré 5.1.6**: not checkable. Replaced by the chapter, whose title Crossref confirms.
