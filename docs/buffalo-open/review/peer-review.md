## Summary of the submission

The note is a survey. It lists about twenty questions that Grothendieck asked in the 1973 Buffalo courses (topoi, algebraic theories relative to a class of limits, accessible categories, algebraic geometry, group schemes), each with a timestamp, his framing and its status today. A few short arguments of its own are marked (e).

## Overall assessment

The survey is useful and mostly accurate. Its links to the recordings make it checkable. The review found three substantive problems: a status left at "probably", an imprecise attribution and an unsupported inference. It also found several attribution slips against the recordings (which course a session belongs to, the number of videos, who raised a counterexample). All have been fixed in the manuscript. The (e) arguments are correct after the fixes listed below.

## Major comments

**M1. Problem 1, the status was left at "probably false".**
*Location:* abstract, Problem 1 status, table, closing paragraph.
*Observation:* The counterexample relied on Sh([0,1]_Scott) being quasi-injective "[jj]", with a note that this should be checked. JJ could not be opened, and no statement of it was found.
*Why it matters:* the main negative claim on Problem 1 rested on an unchecked citation.
*Action taken:* we added a direct proof that the topos is a retract of [[0,1], Set] (e). That is the definition of quasi-injective recalled in LW §1.4, after which LW 1.5.4 and 1.5.9 apply. The status is now "False", and "completely prime" became "completely join-prime", with the reason given. The author should decide whether he accepts an own argument in place of a printed source. If he does not, revert to "probably" and say that a printed statement of quasi-injectivity is missing.

**M2. Problem 2, "local geometric morphisms".**
*Observation:* the Sierpiński open point j: Set → Sh(S) is not a local geometric morphism: its j_* = γ^* has the right adjoint Γ = γ_*, so the local map is γ: Sh(S) → Set. The examples Ĉ → Set (C with a terminal object) are local maps, because Ĉ is a local topos.
*Action taken:* the sentence was rewritten. "His own test case" became "the case he was testing", since at 4:05:50 he discusses dense and open immersions.

**M3. Problem 9, "not decided by ZFC" was unsupported.**
*Observation:* The positive side under VP is fine, if lightly sourced. The negative side argued that "in a model of WVP without VP some such reflective subcategory is not locally presentable". No source was given, and the step needs the converse "¬VP ⇒ some limit-closed full subcategory of an lp category is not lp", which we did not find in print. The ART results (zbMATH review read) concern reflectivity, not accessibility.
*Action taken:* the status is now "yes under an extra hypothesis (accessible inclusion, [AR 2.78] for an lp ambient category; or VP, [AR ch. 6]); in ZFC alone not found settled". The route through Wilson is kept as a pointer only. The abstract, the table and the closing paragraph were adjusted.
