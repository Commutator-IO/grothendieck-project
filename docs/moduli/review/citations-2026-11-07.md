## Claims

- **Voight 2016.** The paper does study the product (monoid) of quadratic rings over any base scheme. Construction 3.12: for x^2 = tx − n and y^2 = sy − m, S ∗ T = R ⊕ Rw with w^2 = (st)w − (mt^2 + ns^2 − 4nm). In the folder-83 convention (b, c, b', c') this is exactly Q(w) = 0, and in the folder-84 convention it is the product of Proposition 2.2 (after U ↦ −U). Voight credits the free affine formula to Hahn (Exercises 14–20, pp. 42–43). Theorem 3.24 characterises the monoid uniquely. Part (iii) identifies S ∗ T with the fixed subring of σ ⊗ τ for *separable* S, T. Lemma 3.14 gives the identity R × R and the absorbing element R[x]/(x^2). The classification of free quadratic algebras by discriminants is in §4 (Theorem 4.3, the Artin–Schreier action on the fibres of disc). The article now cites Construction 3.12 and Theorem 3.24, says that (iii) assumes separability, and explains that Theorem 2.4 weakens this to "one of the two is étale above 2".
- **Beauville 2010.** Proposition 1.1 (classical, after Serre): for a finite group of order prime to char K, PGL2(K) contains A4 and S4 iff −1 is a sum of two squares in K, and contains A5 iff in addition 5 is a square in K. The article now uses it in two places: to explain why Statement 4.3 needs forms of PGL2 (no A5 in PGL2(R)), and as the field-level context for folder 64. The hypothesis "order prime to the characteristic" is stated. In characteristic 5, the case of Remark 4.5, the hypothesis does not hold for A5.
- **Antieau–Meier 2020.** §4 (Definition 4.4, Proposition 4.5 in the arXiv numbering) defines the Legendre parameter space X = A^1_{Z[1/2]} − {0, 1} and proves that X → M(2) is a C2-torsor, so that M(2) ≃ BC_{2,X}. The old sentence, "for the Brauer group and related structures ... see", was vague and cited the paper for its title. It now says what the paper actually contains and keeps the arXiv numbering caveat. Antieau and Meier do not have the μ4-twisted pinning of folder 64, and the article no longer suggests that they do.
- **Hasse–Schmidt 1937** is used only for the divided derivatives D^(i). Appropriate.
- **Knus 1991** is used only as a general reference for quadratic algebras over rings. Appropriate.

Nothing had to be removed. One sentence (Antieau–Meier) was made specific, and two (Voight, Beauville) were made more precise.
