Reviewed file: `transcripts/1/1.modern.tex` (3338 lines, revision of 2026-11-24). I checked it against `transcripts/1/batch-01.fr.tex` to `batch-08.fr.tex` (pp. 1-149). The only online lookups were bibliographic metadata: Numdam, Crossref and the arXiv API. The accountable human reviewer should check every location cited below against the files before acting on it.

## Summary verdict

A very rich and, on the whole, mathematically careful reading. Its many editorial counterexamples are almost all correct, and I checked them:

- f* + g* (p. 7), the fg example (p. 10), A = ½·1 and e_12 (p. 30), (0,2)/(1,1) and (0,0)/(1,−1) (pp. 81, 85), u = 3·1 (p. 79), λ = (10,1) (p. 148), the weighted cyclic shift (p. 143), the compact weighted shift (p. 127).

The core theory also holds up: Prop. 6, Th. 1 of p. 31, the Weyl lemma, the three convexity theorems, the Schatten duality, the Lemma of p. 110 by three rotations, the coefficientwise system of pp. 71-73, the analytic proofs of pp. 126-139, and the repaired lemma of p. 147. The Lean declarations named exist.

Three points need correction:

1. The footnote presenting pp. 70/78-79/110 as Grothendieck's own proof of Lidskii's theorem (1959) goes beyond what the pages establish.
2. A footnote states a false inequality for p < 1.
3. The counterexample to page 28 does not satisfy the hypothesis.

Counts: 3 major, 16 minor.

## Major issues

### M1. Lidskii's theorem attributed to the carton: an assembled, partly struck argument presented as « la sienne », with one step asserted and an unverified negative claim about *La théorie de Fredholm*

- **Location:** §« Puissances extérieures d'opérateurs à trace, mise au net », Théorème 1 and its footnote (« C'est le théorème de la trace, publié par Lidskii en 1959. *La théorie de Fredholm* (1956) … ne le contient pas. La démonstration de ces pages, biffée page 79 et complétée par la proposition de la page 110, est la sienne … »). Also §« Fonctions entières » (« la démonstration du théorème 1 de la page 70, que la proposition de la page 110 achève »).
- **Observation:**
  1. The pieces exist on the pages:
     - (18) |α_n(u)| ≤ α_n(|u|), p. 78;
     - the summability Σ|λ_i| < ∞, Weyl, p. 88;
     - the Proposition of pp. 110-111, whose three-rotation proof I checked and which is correct.

     But the only place where they are put together is p. 79, entirely struck through. That page also contains the false product bound (as the reading notes).
  2. The step « les inverses des zéros de det(1 − zu) sont les z_i » (eigenvalues, with algebraic multiplicities) is asserted on p. 79 and in the reading without argument. It is classical Fredholm theory, but it is a necessary input and should be named as such.
  3. « *La théorie de Fredholm* … ne le contient pas » and « exposé que Grothendieck dit lui-même didactique » are given without a page reference.
     - Numdam confirms only the metadata of the Bourbaki exposé « La théorie de Fredholm » (n° 91, 1954, pp. 377-384), not the content of the 1956 Bull. SMF memoir.
     - The claim, combined with « est la sienne », amounts to a priority statement (a 1953 proof of a 1959 theorem). The edition's own practice avoids such statements.
- **Evidence:** batch-04, p. 78 (note: « la moitié inférieure de la page … est barrée de deux grands traits obliques, ainsi que toute la page 79 »), and p. 79 (« Les inverses des zéros de det(1 − zu) sont les z_i … »). batch-06, pp. 110-111.
- **Why it matters:** This is the reading's most consequential historical claim. Everything else in the footnote (Simon's later route) can stand only if the attribution is stated exactly.
- **Proposed fix:** Rewrite the footnote along these lines.
  - « Les pages contiennent les ingrédients d'une démonstration : majoration (18), Σ|λ_i| ≤ ‖u‖_1 (p. 88), proposition des pp. 110-111. La page 79, qui les assemble, est biffée, et l'identification des zéros de det(1 − zu) aux inverses des valeurs propres y est admise (théorie de Fredholm classique). »
  - Drop « est la sienne », or restrict it to the Proposition of p. 110.
  - Either give the exact place in *La théorie de Fredholm* (Bull. SMF 84, 1956) that shows the result is absent, together with the source of « didactique », or remove both clauses.
  - Keep Simon (1977) as a comparison of method, not of priority.

### M2. False inequality in the footnote to the Théorème of pp. 146-147: Σ|s_ν(A) − s_ν(B)|^p ≤ S_p(A − B) fails for p < 1

- **Location:** §« Les inégalités de Ky Fan, et les petites valeurs singulières », footnote to the Théorème (« pour p < 1 l'inégalité Σ|s_ν(A) − s_ν(B)|^p ≤ S_p(A − B) est vraie aussi »).
- **Observation:** Counterexample with A = diag(2, 1) and B = A + vv^*, v = (1, 1):
  - B = [[3, 1], [1, 2]] has eigenvalues (5 ± √5)/2 ≈ 3.618 and 1.382;
  - A − B = −vv^* has singular values (2, 0).

  For p = ½, the left side is √1.618 + √0.382 ≈ 1.890, while the right side is √2 ≈ 1.414. For p → 0 the left side tends to 2, the number of shifted singular values, and the right side to 1, the rank of A − B. Rank-one perturbations can shift all n singular values, so no constant C_p rescues the inequality either.
- **Why it matters:** It is a false mathematical statement in the edition's voice. The theorem itself is true, and so is the lemma the reading proves.
- **Proposed fix:** Delete the clause. Keep Mirsky for p ≥ 1. For p < 1, say that the theorem (convergence of the singular value sequences in ℓ^p) is what the lemma gives, and that the Mirsky-type inequality has no analogue for p < 1.

### M3. Page 28 departure: the counterexample to « Δ(A) < ∞ ⟹ B ∈ 𝔞 » violates the hypothesis B ∈ 𝔟

- **Location:** §« Le déterminant (pages 27 à 32) », item b) and its footnote. Also the header line « page 28: "Δ(A) < ∞ ⟺ B ∈ a" holds only as ⇐ (counterexample given) ».
- **Observation:**
  - The footnote takes A = 1 − ½E with E a projection of infinite rank and corank. But B = −½E is not in 𝔟 (in L(H), 𝔟 is the compact operators), while item b) assumes B ∈ 𝔟.
  - The departure itself is real. Take B = −½·Σ (1/n)·ē_n ⊗ e_n, which is compact, hermitian, not trace class, and has 1 + B ≥ 0. Then φ_{|1+B|} ≡ 1, so Δ(1 + B) = 1, yet B ∉ 𝔞.
- **Why it matters:** The edition's list of substantive departures rests on an invalid example.
- **Proposed fix:** Replace the example. The rest of the footnote, the case B ≥ 0, is correct.

## Minor issues

1. **p. 7 rules:** « φ_{f+g} ≤ φ_f + φ_g ; φ_{fg} ≤ φ_f·φ_g » are stated in the body as pointwise rules. The footnote rightly says that pointwise they are false. Put the integrated (sub-majorisation) form in the body, or the pointwise form with shifted arguments, (f+g)*(s+t) ≤ f*(s) + g*(t).
2. **p. 10 footnote:** in the counterexample φ_f·φ_g behaves like 1/s, not 1/(2s) (f* = g* = s^{−1/2} on ]0, ½[). This is a trivial slip.
3. **p. 11:**
   - « fermé pour la norme de L^1 (a fortiori pour σ(L^∞, L^1)) » skips a step. A norm-closed set is weak*-closed here only because it is *convex* (Mazur), and weak* convergence implies σ(L^1, L^∞) convergence on bounded sets.
   - The footnote identifies Banach-Dieudonné with Krein-Šmulian, which are distinct theorems. Name the one used (Krein-Šmulian) and mention the other as related.
4. **p. 15 footnote, « deux ans avant Lorentz »:** unsourced. Lorentz's papers on rearrangement-invariant spaces date from 1950-51, before 1953. Give the reference intended or drop it.
5. **Prop. 7 (p. 27):** « les deux mesures sont alors, hors de 0, de masse finie » is false for A ∈ 𝔞, as the reading's own footnote at p. 22 shows (A = Σ n^{−2}·ē_n ⊗ e_n). Use the finite measures x·dμ on [0, ‖A‖^2], which are determined by their moments.
6. **Corollaire 5, p. 125:** the body says « avec |UA| = |A| sur le support », but the footnote correctly says |UA| ≤ |A|. Align the body with the footnote.
7. **Hölder, p. 137:** the display « (λμ)^r·S_r(AB) ≤ (λ^p/p)·S_p(A) + (μ^q/q)·S_q(B) » lacks the factor r. It should read (r/p)·λ^p·S_p(A) + (r/q)·μ^q·S_q(B), which is what the next line, with α = r/p and β = r/q, uses.
8. **Quotation, p. 143:** « Majorations individuelles, p. ex. |λ_i(u)| ≤ s_{i/2}(u) ou analogue ? ». The page writes ρ_{i/2}(u). Keep the page's letter inside guillemets, or say that the notation is changed.
9. **Quotation, p. 73:** « les inégalités les plus fortes possibles » is presented as the page's judgement. The page says the system « semblent les inégalités les plus fortes possibles ». Restore « semblent ».
10. **Résumé:** « « est-ce utile ? » et « pas d'importance » en marge de deux corollaires ». « Est-ce utile ? » is marginal to Corollaire 4 of p. 91. « Pas d'importance » answers the question on uniform convexity on p. 145, not a corollary.
11. **mathlib PRs:** the footnote to Théorème 2 (pp. 71-73) says mathlib4 PRs 44757 and 44759 are « ouvertes ». `gh pr view` shows both are CLOSED, and under the project's rule for the Lean article closed PRs are dropped. Update the footnote.
12. **Lean paths stale:** `lean/Grothendieck/LinearAlgebra/Folder1.lean` (cited three times, and in the header) does not exist in the project. The declarations are in `~/Commutator/GrothendieckArchivesProofs/Grothendieck/LinearAlgebra/Folder1.lean`.
13. **p. 146 footnote:** « L'inégalité en norme d'opérateur ‖H^r − K^r‖ ≤ ‖H − K‖^r … de Kittaneh en général » is a questionable attribution. The general 0 < r ≤ 1 case follows from operator monotonicity and is at least as old as Birman-Koplienko-Solomyak (1975) and Ando. Check it or soften it.
14. **Final footnote of § p. 33 Corollaire 3:** the formula Φ_{|A|}(t) = sup |τ(BA)| is said to remain true without the non-atomic hypothesis « par le plongement des pages 25 et 34 ». But that plongement supposes a finite trace (as the reading notes for Th. 1). State it for a finite trace, or justify the semi-finite case.
15. **Multiplicativity (p. 98):** N(vu) ≤ N′(u)·N″(v) is derived « par le corollaire 1 du théorème 3 ». It is Théorème 3 itself (applied with f = N), not Corollaire 1, which concerns sums Σf.
16. **Prose:** « ce qu'on en donne est ce que fournit le B = EU^* » (footnote, p. 33) is a pseudo-cleft. Suggested: « on en donne ce que fournit ». The other « ce que » occurrences are relative clauses and acceptable.
