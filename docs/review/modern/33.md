# Review of the modernised reading of folder 33 (`transcripts/33/33.modern.tex`)

*Working draft. This is a simulated referee report that the author asked for on his own unpublished work. The reading was compared, page by page, with `transcripts/33/batch-01.fr.tex` … `batch-09.fr.tex` (pp. 1-180), with local scripts only. Bibliographic metadata was looked up on Crossref. No manuscript text was sent to any outside service. The reading itself was not edited.*

## Summary verdict

The reading is careful and, on the whole, mathematically sound. I re-derived most of its corrections and they hold. These include the étale trace f_!f^*F → F (p. 19), the twist T_{Y/X}^{-1}[-2d] in relative purity (p. 55), Ri^!(T_{X/S}) = T_{Y/S}[-2d] (p. 73), the inverse twist on p. 79, the variance fix of the first projection formula (p. 89), and the conversion from homological to cohomological Tor-amplitude in § (31), which matches the page's [N-2d, M]. The proper-support counit in § (32) (p. 159) and the counterexample to statement (i) of p. 171 are also right.

The reading has two problems serious enough to fix before release:

- One footnote "corrects" a hypothesis the page has right. The corrected statement is true but weaker than the page's, and the footnote's claim that the change is necessary is false.
- Some quotations in the Résumé and the spine are presented as firm readings, but the transcription marks their key words \uncertain or leaves them illegible. One of them carries a judgement about the author.

There are also about a dozen minor points: small gaps in sketched arguments, a few quotations that are not literal, and two hypotheses the reading changed without a footnote.

## Major issues

### M1. Künneth special case: the "same side" correction is wrong (p. 115)

- **Location:** § « Formule de projection et formule de Künneth », the *Cas particulier* and its footnote (lines 1043-1052 of the reading).
- **Observation:** The page's hypothesis is that F or G is flat, and that the R^i_!f(F) or the R^j_!(G) are flat, with two independent "ou". The reading asks for the flatness of F and of its R^i f_!F together, on the same side. The footnote says this is necessary, because "c'est la platitude de ce côté qui permet de le représenter par un complexe plat borné à cohomologie plate".
- **Evidence:** The two hypotheses do different jobs.
  - The flatness of F or of G makes the ordinary ⊗ on Z = X ×_S Y equal to ⊗^L. Then the left side is R^m h_!(p_1^*F ⊗^L p_2^*G), which the Künneth corollary identifies with H^m(Rf_!F ⊗^L Rg_!G).
  - The flatness of the cohomology of one of Rf_!F, Rg_!G kills the Tor terms. Say all R^j g_!G are flat. Then Rg_!G is a bounded complex with flat cohomology, so it has finite Tor-amplitude. By the reading's own remark on p. 139, it is quasi-isomorphic to a bounded complex N of flat sheaves. Descending induction gives flat cycles and boundaries for N. The Künneth theorem for complexes (applied stalkwise) then yields H^m(A ⊗ N) ≅ ⊕ H^i(A) ⊗ H^j(N) for any A, because Tor_1(H(A), H(N)) = 0.

  So "F flat and all R^j g_!G flat" is enough, and so is any other combination. The page's statement is correct as written.
- **Why it matters:** A footnote that calls a correct statement of Grothendieck wrong is the most damaging kind of error in a modernised reading. The reader is told the page has a mistake when it does not.
- **Proposed fix:** State the page's hypothesis: F or G flat, and the R^i f_!F or the R^j g_!G flat, the two choices independent. Replace the footnote by a short justification, along the lines of: « la platitude de F ou de G rend le ⊗ dérivé ; la platitude des faisceaux de cohomologie d'un des deux côtés annule les Tor ». Remove the line about this departure from the header list.

### M2. Quotations resting on uncertain or illegible readings are presented as firm (Résumé; pp. 57, 59, 65, 121)

- **Location:** Résumé, third and fourth paragraphs (lines 123-131), and the spine's "Un choix de route" section (lines 1078-1085). Also the § 23 sentence on Hartshorne (line 699), and the header lines 58-59.
- **Observation:**
  - **p. 57:** in « ce que je n'ai pas fait », the transcription marks both « n'ai » and « fait » \uncertain.
  - **p. 59:** in « me semble nullement clair », « nullement » is \uncertain.
  - **p. 121:** « cuite et pressée » is \uncertain as a whole. In « Étant parti dans un formulaire de dualité général, je préfère… », the words « formulaire », « général » and « préfère » are \uncertain.
  - **p. 121:** the word after « Lefschetz- » is \ill{}. The reading prints it as « Lefschetz--Verdier » without saying so.
  - **p. 65:** « Hartshorne » is \uncertain, yet the reading writes « L'argument, dit-il, est dû à Hartshorne ».

  The Résumé builds a judgement on the p. 57 sentence (« une honnêteté de travail qui vaut d'être relevée »). The footnote on p. 57 says the reading restores only what the pages say « clairement », but these words are not clear on the page.
- **Why it matters:** The brief requires every quotation attributed to a page to match the batch files. These quotations match the words, but not the level of certainty. A reader who cites them will cite conjectural readings as Grothendieck's words.
- **Proposed fix:** Pick one convention and apply it. One option is a footnote at the first quotation of each passage, saying the transcription marks the words as uncertain. Another is to avoid quoting and paraphrase instead. For p. 121, write « Lefschetz-[mot illisible] », or footnote the restored « Verdier ». For p. 65, write « dû, semble-t-il, à Hartshorne ». Consider softening the Résumé's judgement, or stating that it rests on an uncertain reading.

## Minor issues

1. **p. 27, change of hypothesis not footnoted.**
   - Observation: The page sends the class into cohomology with proper supports when « X est propre sur un S ». The reading says « si Y est propre sur une base S ». The weaker hypothesis is the correct one, but the footnote at line 456 mentions only the missing ⊗d.
   - Fix: Add that the page assumes X proper.
2. **p. 47, « lisse » added silently.**
   - Observation: The page says only « Si F loc. ct. » before the twisted Poincaré formula. The reading adds « si X est lisse ». The addition is needed, because μ_n^{⊗d} with d = dim X only makes sense for smooth X.
   - Fix: Footnote it.
3. **Quotations that are not literal.** Each of these should be quoted literally or turned into a paraphrase.
   - p. 13: the reading has « et il ne faut surtout pas les confondre »; the page has « Il ne faut… », with no « et ».
   - p. 13: the reading has « immédiatement »; the page has « Dém. pureté immédiate ».
   - p. 19: the reading has « identiques à celles des R^q f_* quand f est propre »; the page has « aussi bien que si f propre », and « identiques » is \uncertain.
   - p. 47: the reading has « d'Artin »; the page has « de Artin ».
   - Header comment, line 39: the reading has « compatibilité des deux traces »; the page has « Compatibilité avec les deux traces ».
4. **p. 81, Gysin map in ordinary cohomology.**
   - Observation: The reading derives the maps « en appliquant Rg_! » and then states H^{q+2δ}(X, h^*F)(δ) → H^q(Y, g^*F) « si f est propre et Z le spectre d'un corps ». Applying Rg_! only gives cohomology with proper supports. The ordinary-cohomology map comes from applying Rg_* (or RΓ) to the trace Rf_*Rf^! → id, which uses only the properness of f. The added hypothesis « Z le spectre d'un corps » is not on the page and is not needed.
   - Fix: Say « en appliquant RΓ(Y, −) » and drop the field hypothesis, or footnote why it is there.
5. **5° (p. 93), the « argument formel ».**
   - Observation: The Yoneda argument identifies only the two functors K ↦ Hom(K, −) on test objects in D^-(X), while the targets lie in D^+(X). To get an isomorphism of objects, one should first construct the canonical map, then check it on K = j_!Λ_U[n]. The result itself is standard (SGA 4 XVIII 3.1.12.2; reference not re-checked here).
   - Fix: Write « on construit la flèche canonique et on la teste sur les j_!Λ_U[n] ».
6. **§ (30) (pp. 133-135), duality of Hom-groups.**
   - Observation: The reading says RHom(L, M) and RHom(M, L) are dual « par l'accouplement » on Hom_{D(A)}. Over a general commutative ring A, the duality holds at the level of complexes. It passes to H^0 only when Hom_A(−, A) is exact, for example when A = Z/n is self-injective.
   - Fix: Add that restriction.
7. **pp. 109-111, cohomological-dimension footnote.**
   - Observation: The footnote says that for RΓ, « c'est-à-dire f à valeurs dans Spec k », one needs cd(k) finite. In fact f_* for f : X → Spec k, with values in étale sheaves on Spec k, has finite cohomological dimension whatever k is. RΓ is Γ(k, −) composed with f_*, so it is RΓ, not f_*, that needs cd(k) finite. Meanwhile, the main text gives as the reason « la finitude de la dimension cohomologique des corps de type fini », which is false for Z/2 over Q.
   - Fix: Separate the two points. f_* has finite cd for f of finite type, because the fields that matter are fields over separably closed ones. RΓ over a non-separably-closed field may not.
8. **Spine, which pages carry his hand (lines 146-150 and header lines 18-19).**
   - Observation: The list of « exceptions » (164, 165, 166, 169, 171, …, 179) mixes odd pages with even ones. The real exceptions are the even pages 2, 164 and 166 (and the cover p. 163), and the odd page 167, which is a typescript (batch-09 header).
   - Fix: Reword, e.g. « toutes les pages impaires sauf 167, plus 2, 164 et 166 ».
9. **4° (p. 91), the formula Rg^!Rf_* ≃ Rf'_*Rg'^!.**
   - Observation: The reading labels it « (toujours) ». The page attaches « g lissifiable » to it.
   - Fix: Write « (g lissifiable) ».
10. **3° (p. 89), tensor projection formula.** The page says « G loc. libre », and the reading adds « de type fini ». This is harmless, but it is not on the page.
11. **Résumé scale.** « sur cent quatre-vingts pages » is accurate as a page count. However, about ninety of those pages carry his hand, which the spine itself says.
12. **§ (32), local terms footnote.** Consider naming Pink (Ann. of Math. 135, 1992, verified on Crossref). He proved Deligne's conjecture under resolution of singularities, before Fujiwara. This is optional.
