## Summary verdict

The reading is careful, well organised and mostly faithful. Its notation conventions (X' reserved for the punctured spectrum; W, P, B for the page's other X', X', Y) are explicit and applied consistently. Almost every departure from the page is footnoted. The counterexamples it adds are correct: two 3-planes meeting at a point, the number-field covering for Th. 2, the skyscraper for B_n, the k[[x,y,z]] example for the non-fullness of rho, and the cuspidal cubic. Parts II (Remarques 1-3, conclusion pratique, pi_0/pi_1/Pic) and III (the duality chains (*), B_n, A'_i, A^f_n, C'_i, C_n) were checked step by step and hold.

The main weakness is three central statements that the reading asserts as true but does not establish, as the brief requires:

1. the local connectedness theorem, whose proof rests on two lemmas left unproved, one of them justified circularly;
2. the Hom-comparison theorem of p. 45, whose own Lean footnote shows it needs an extra hypothesis;
3. Théorème 2, whose proof the reading itself says it has not checked.

Each can be fixed cheaply, either by citing a precise source or by restricting what is asserted. The other points are minor: quotation hygiene, a few page descriptions, and two hypotheses missing in Part IV.

Counts: 3 major, 16 minor.

## Major issues

### M1. Local connectedness theorem (pp. 8-12): the proof rests on unproved Lemmas 2 and 3, and Lemma 3 is justified circularly

- **Location:** §I, "Deux lemmes sur le spectre épointé" (Lemme 2, Lemme 3 and the footnote to Lemme 3); "Le théorème de connexité local", Corollaire 1 and its "Démonstration".
- **Observation:**
  - Lemma 2 (the case k = 1, l = 1, which is the real content of the theorem) gets no proof, only the Krull dimension count. The reading says so: "La démonstration ne se lit qu'en partie".
  - The proof of Lemma 3 is not reconstructed either. The footnote says that the choice of Phi "demande plus que (S_2)".
  - The same footnote then affirms Lemma 3 on the grounds that it "est de toute façon contenu dans le théorème qui suit". But the theorem's proof (Corollaire 1, the step k-1 -> k) invokes Lemma 3. As written, the theorem is proved from Lemma 3 and Lemma 3 is justified by the theorem.
- **Evidence:** In the modern file, the footnote to Lemme 3 ends "le lemme en est l'étape de récurrence", and the Corollaire 1 proof says "Soit Phi la partie finie que donne le Lemme 3". On the page (p. 6, batch-01), Lemma 3 is argued independently: normal implies (S_2), Phi chosen so that Y' stays (S_2), then Hartshorne. So the page is not circular. The reading becomes circular because it drops that argument and substitutes the theorem.
- **Why it matters:** This is the folder's headline theorem, and the "Démonstration" reads as a complete proof when it is not. The statement itself is true: it is the local form of Grothendieck's connectedness theorem.
- **Proposed fix:** Pick one of the following.
  - Present the theorem as known: Grothendieck's connectedness theorem in the local form of SGA 2 XIII (give the exact number after checking it). Label the induction as "the page's plan of proof, given Lemmas 2 and 3", with Lemma 2 cited and Lemma 3 stated as the page's, its proof only outlined.
  - Supply a proof of Lemma 2, which is the only genuinely hard input. Then rewrite the Lemma 3 footnote so that it does not lean on the theorem.

  In either case, delete the clause "il est de toute façon contenu dans le théorème qui suit".

### M2. Hom comparison on the punctured spectrum (p. 45) is asserted without proof, and the edition's own Lean work shows that more is needed

- **Location:** §II, "Une forme locale (pages 45 à 51)", the displayed bijection Hom(F, G) -> Hom(F^, G^), the Lean footnote attached to it, and the following paragraph on the dévissage.
- **Observation:**
  - The statement, "if G has depth >= 2 at the closed points of X' - Y', then Hom(F, G) -> Hom(F^, G^) is bijective", is given in the reading's own voice, under the convention that what it states is true.
  - Its proof is not given. The reading follows the page's dévissage, which "s'arrête là" at the « ? » of p. 47, and the t-regular case "reste à faire".
  - The Lean footnote says plainly: "L'énoncé est ramené, non démontré". Per that footnote, the reduction to Stacks 0EJ7 requires the strict (1,2)-inequalities for G^ at the points of Y', "où la page ne demande rien".
  - No literature reference is offered.
- **Evidence:** I checked `GrothendieckArchivesProofs/Grothendieck/AlgebraicGeometry/Folder58DepthComparison.lean`. In `hom_bijective_of_depth_two_off_V_t` (line 76), `hstrict` is an explicit hypothesis. I also checked Stacks 0EJ7 online (Lemma 52.19.6). It needs A I-adically complete with a dualizing complex, cd(A, I) <= d, the strict (1, 1+d)-inequalities for the completion (0EJ3: a condition at the points y of U ∩ Y), and depth >= 2 off the formal locus.
- **Why it matters:** A reading that is required to be correct as it stands presents, as its own statement, something that neither it nor its formalisation proves. The edition's sources only support a weaker statement, the one with the extra condition at the points of Y'. I could not settle whether the page's version (no condition on Y') is true in general.
- **Proposed fix:** Do one of the following.
  - Give a precise reference that proves exactly this statement. SGA 2 X or XII is worth checking: there the Lef condition for an open U ⊃ Y is obtained from depth >= 2 at the closed points of U - Y.
  - Restate it as "the page asserts", with the reading claiming only the version under the 0EJ7 hypotheses.
  - Add the missing hypothesis on G^ along Y'.

  Do the same for the sentence "En particulier rho est pleinement fidèle sur la sous-catégorie…", which depends on it.

### M3. Théorème 2 (pp. 22-26) is stated as true, its proof is only partly checked, and Lemme 5 is asserted at points where it is not argued

- **Location:** §I, "Les fibres spéciales d'un morphisme propre", Théorème 2, Lemme 5, the sketch, and the footnote "Nous n'avons pas vérifié l'esquisse dans tous ses détails".
- **Observation:**
  - Lemma 5 is stated for every x in X_0, with bound k - 1 - dim closure(x). The sketch only treats closed points x, where it gives k - 1.
  - The non-closed case, which the reading's Lemma 4 consumes "en tout point", is not argued.
  - The reading admits that the sketch is unverified. It nonetheless states the theorem "sous les hypothèses où elle nous paraît aller jusqu'au bout".
- **Evidence:** In the modern file, the Lemme 5 footnote says "nous donnons la borne, en tout point, que le Lemme 4, tel que nous l'énonçons, consomme", and the sketch says "ce que le Lemme 5 demande en un point fermé". On the page (p. 24), Lemma 5 is stated « pour tt pt fermé x de X_0 ».
- **Why it matters:** A central statement is asserted on an admittedly incomplete argument.
- **Proposed fix:** The gap is easy to close.
  - X_0 is of finite type over a field, so every non-empty closed subset has a closed point. The proof of Lemma 4 then goes through with z closed and the hypothesis only at closed points: "punctured spectrum connected in dim >= k-1". The reading's own footnote to Lemma 4 already notes that z closed suffices.
  - Restate Lemma 4 in that closed-point form, Lemma 5 at closed points only, as on the page, and check the remaining step: the passage to the normalisation via excellence, with the image of a space connected in dim >= k under a finite surjection.
  - Alternatively, cite the published statement (SGA 2 XIII, or Fulton-Lazarsfeld §2) and downgrade the sketch to a gloss.

## Minor issues

m1. **Corollaire 3 (projective form):**
- **Location:** the paragraph "On le déduit du théorème local…".
- **Observation:** Only the components of the completed cone are treated. Hypothesis (ii), connectedness in dim >= k+1 of the completion's punctured spectrum, is not transferred.
- **Fix:** Add one sentence. A graded domain has a completed local ring that is a domain (look at lowest-degree forms). Intersections of components complete to cones over the intersections, so the chain criterion for connectedness in dim >= k carries over.
