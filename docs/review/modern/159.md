# Review of the modernised reading of folder 159 (working draft)

Reviewed file: `transcripts/159/159.modern.tex` (revision of 2026-11-24). I checked it against `transcripts/159/batch-01.fr.tex` (pp. 1-20) and `batch-02.fr.tex` (pp. 21-33). This is a simulated referee report. The author asked for it, and it was produced locally. No manuscript text was sent to any external service. The only online lookups were bibliographic metadata: the arXiv API and Crossref. The accountable human reviewer should check every location cited below against the files before acting on it.

## Summary verdict

The reading is ambitious and largely well founded.

- The dictionary of pages 1-6 checks out: Aff(r), the action u_a, the degree rules, and the class -n·a·f on J^n/J^{n+2}.
- So do the de Rham and Kodaira-Spencer pages, the quadric count (1/2)(g-2)(g-3), the Euler-Poincaré computation of page 15, the degree argument of page 21 as an argument, and the abstract group lemma of page 31.
- Its treatment of the 6g-6 / 3g-3 anomaly on page 15 is exemplary. It says what coherence requires and leaves the page as it stands.
- The added Klein-group example on page 14 is correct: V_4 is self-centralising in PGL_2.

Five places fall short of the brief's requirement that the reading be "mathematically correct as it stands":

1. The theorem of page 21 is false as stated, because "réduction" must be horizontal.
2. The reading endorses the characterisation of page 17 as "juste", but it is not.
3. The page 15 N.B. is reproduced with the wrong condition (c) for finite automorphisms, and representability is asserted where the page only conjectures it.
4. The symbol of the connection on gr^i (pp. 32-33) is multiplication by i·ϖ_0, not by ϖ_0.
5. The rigidity computation (pp. 27-30) is misstated for n = 2, and its existence half is never argued.

All five can be fixed locally.

Counts: 5 major, 17 minor.

## Major issues

### M1. Theorem of page 21: "ne se réduit à aucun sous-groupe" is false unless the reduction is horizontal, and deg M = 0 is assumed silently

- **Location:** §« Aucune réduction du groupe structural », the « Le calcul de degrés » paragraph and the boxed Théorème (page 21). Also the Résumé (« le groupe PGL_2 ne se ramène jamais à un sous-groupe plus petit »).
- **Observation:**
  - The theorem and the argument speak of reducing "le groupe structural" without saying that the reduction is compatible with the projective connection (flat, i.e. horizontal).
  - Read literally, the statement is false. The section σ that is part of the data (P, σ) is itself a reduction of the PGL_2-bundle to a Borel subgroup.
  - The degree argument also uses deg(det F) = deg M = 0. That holds only because the reduction is flat, so that M is a flat line bundle. The reading states it as a bare fact.
- **Evidence:** batch-02, p. 21 writes « donc (\uncertain{lorsque} M est de degré 0) (2 deg L = deg ω_X) ». The degree-zero condition is conditional on the page, and the reading drops that.
  - For σ itself: E ≃ F ⊗ L with F = E, L = O_X, and det F = ω has degree 2g-2 ≠ 0. So the argument rightly does not apply to σ, but the statement does.
- **Why it matters:** This is the result the Résumé calls « le résultat le plus net du dossier ». The Zariski-density corollary of page 22 also needs it in its horizontal form.
- **Proposed fix:**
  - State the theorem for reductions of the bundle *with its connection* (horizontal reductions, equivalently reductions of the monodromy over C).
  - Justify deg M = 0 by flatness, since a flat line bundle on a proper curve in characteristic 0 has degree 0.
  - Footnote that the page writes « lorsque M est de degré 0 », and adjust the Résumé phrase in the same way.

### M2. Page 17: the characterisation of the uniformising locus is called « juste », but it is not, and the scope of « on suppose » is misdescribed

- **Location:** §« Le tableau analytique », the paragraph « La colonne de droite du même feuillet caractérise l'image … ». Also §« Ce qui ne s'articule pas », 2nd bullet (« La caractérisation est juste, mais le feuillet l'introduit par « on suppose » »), and the Résumé (« décrit le lieu des structures qui uniformisent, et introduit la description par le mot « on suppose » »).
- **Observation:**
  1. Mathematics.
     - "ρ injective with an invariant domain D ⊂ P^1(C) on which π acts freely with compact quotient (D unique)" does not characterise the image of Teichmüller space under Fuchsian uniformisation. Quasi-Fuchsian groups, and also the degenerate groups, have such an invariant component.
     - D is never unique. A Fuchsian group preserves both half-planes, and a quasi-Fuchsian group both components of the domain of discontinuity.
     - The Fuchsian locus is the set of representations conjugate into PSL_2(R) (equivalently, D a round disc, one of the two). It is a real-analytic, totally real submanifold, so « morphisme analytique » M_g(X) → M_g^z(X) is real-analytic only.
  2. Fidelity. In the transcription the sentence opened by « On suppose » is « On suppose que l'image … par … constructible … algébrique. » The description « Elle est formée des Hom injectifs … (il sera unique) … quotient compact » is a separate, assertive sentence. So the page does not hedge the characterisation, and the reading does not flag that it is wrong.
- **Evidence:** batch-01, p. 17, right-hand column (lines 857-863). The standard references are Bers' simultaneous uniformisation and Maskit on groups with an invariant component. The reading's own footnote source (Dumas, arXiv:0902.1951) also treats the quasi-Fuchsian locus as distinct from the Fuchsian one.
- **Why it matters:** Under the edition's own rule, the true statement must appear and the footnote must say what the page has. Here the reading certifies a false statement.
- **Proposed fix:**
  - State the correct description: Fuchsian representations, D a round disc, real-analytic embedding of T_g.
  - Footnote that the page's condition (invariant domain, free action, compact quotient) also admits quasi-Fuchsian and degenerate groups, and that « il sera unique » is false.
  - Rewrite the Résumé and the « Ce qui ne s'articule pas » sentence so that « on suppose » governs only constructibility and algebraicity.

### M3. Page 15 N.B.: condition c) does not exclude finite automorphisms, and representability is not established

- **Location:** §« Le calcul de dimension », paragraph « De là : la condition d′) signifie que … c) qu'il n'a pas d'automorphismes finis, de sorte que M_g^z est rigide, donc représentable ».
- **Observation:**
  - By the reading's own analysis of page 14, c) is equivalent to b), which says Aut has dimension 0. It is a), Aut = 1, that excludes finite automorphisms. Its own Klein-group example satisfies c) and not a).
  - « donc représentable » turns into a conclusion what the page leaves as a conjecture.
- **Evidence:** batch-01, p. 15, lines 773-782: « c) qu'il n'a pas d'automorphismes finis », then « … représentable ! Le semble. » In the margin: « Sans doute peut-on prouver la représentabilité … ». The text is garbled, but it is plainly a "sans doute".
  - The page also writes « (d'où équivalence de d) et d″)) » after (*). The reading silently corrects this to d′) ⇔ d″), which is right, but there is no footnote.
- **Why it matters:** An internal contradiction between the pages 14 and 15 sections, plus an unproved claim presented as proved.
- **Proposed fix:**
  - Write « a) » (or « a), qui équivaut à c) en genre ≥ 2 par les pages 21-22 »), with a footnote that the page has « c) ».
  - Replace « donc représentable » by « ce qui laisse attendre la représentabilité (« Sans doute peut-on prouver … », en marge) ».
  - Footnote the d) / d′) correction.

### M4. Pages 32-33: the symbol on gr^i is i·ϖ_0, not « un seul et même ϖ_0, indépendant de i »

- **Location:** §« Le cadre, tel que les pages 32 et 33 le posent », the display gr^i ≃ Ω^{⊗i} → Ω^{⊗(i-1)} ⊗ Ω^1_{X/S} and the sentence « cet homomorphisme est la multiplication par un seul et même ϖ_0 … indépendant de i ».
- **Observation:**
  - By the Leibniz rule, C(ω^i) = i·ω^{i-1}·C(ω), whose degree-(i-1) part is i·ω^{i-1}·ϖ_0. So the induced map on gr^i is multiplication by i·ϖ_0.
  - Page 30 itself writes C(λω^{⊗i}) = ω^{⊗i}⊗dλ + λ·i·ω^{⊗(i-1)}…
  - The page's « ϖ_0 … est indépendant de i » is true of the section ϖ_0, but not of the map.
- **Evidence:** batch-02, p. 30, line 411, and pp. 32-33, lines 523-542.
- **Why it matters:** The pages 32-33 frame is stated over an arbitrary X/S. Regularity of the graded map is then equivalent to regularity of ϖ_0 only when i is invertible, which is why characteristic 0 enters. The same factor reappears as (n+1)α_n on page 28.
- **Proposed fix:** Write « multiplication par i·ϖ_0 », and footnote that the page writes ϖ_0. Note that in characteristic 0 (or with i invertible) regularity of C is still equivalent to ϖ_0 : Ω ≅ Ω^1_{X/S}.

### M5. Rigidity computation (pp. 27-30) and Proposition of p. 28: misstated for n = 2, and only the uniqueness half is argued

- **Location:** §« Le calcul du changement de paramètre », the displays C(ω_0^u)^{u^{-1}} = C(ω_0) + (n+1)α_n·ω_0^n·ϖ_0 and « (n+1)α_n = 0 ». The sentence « au-delà du terme de degré 2, aucun changement de paramètre n'est disponible ». Then §« La structure du groupe G », where the surjectivity argument invokes « la proposition de la page 28 ».
- **Observation:**
  1. The hypothesis "α_i = 0 for i < n" with u = 1 + Σ_{i≥2} α_i ω_0^i allows n = 2. For n = 2, however, the new term 3α_2·ω_0^2·ϖ_0 has degree 2, which the form ĉ (components of degrees 0, 1, 2) allows. That term is exactly the quadratic-differential ambiguity, the torsor under Γ(Ω^{⊗2}).
     - The page treats this correctly. On p. 28 the general case assumes α_2 = 0 (« Supposons α_2 = 0, … calculons mod Ω^{n+1} »), and α_2 is computed separately modulo Ω^3.
     - As written, the reading's condition kills α_2 too, which contradicts the torsor it announces.
  2. The computation only proves *uniqueness*: an element that is the identity to order n and preserves the form has α_n = 0. This is the corollary at the top of p. 29.
     - The *existence* statement is never argued: the Proposition of p. 28 (the bijection Conn·G_0 → Conn(Ŝ)) and item b) of p. 25. The standard argument solves for α_n, n ≥ 3, recursively. The new degree-n term (n+1)α_n·ϖ_0·ω_0^n can absorb any degree-n component, because (n+1)ϖ_0 is invertible (regularity and characteristic 0). It must also take care of the dα_{n-1} terms that the reading drops.
     - The reading uses this Proposition in the surjectivity proof without ever stating it. The page's own version of the Proposition (p. 28) lacks the regularity hypothesis, which only appears on pp. 32-33.
- **Evidence:** batch-02, p. 28, lines 320-361.
- **Why it matters:** This is « le résultat principal du dernier tiers du dossier ». The Résumé (« l'isomorphisme existe, il est unique dès qu'on l'a fixé à l'ordre 3 ») rests on it.
- **Proposed fix:**
  - Restrict the condition to n ≥ 3 and say that the n = 2 term is the free ambiguity.
  - State the Proposition of p. 28 with « régulières » (footnoting the page's omission) and give the recursive existence argument in three lines.
  - Say that « modulo Ω^{n+1} » means modulo J^{n+1}.

## Minor issues

1. **G(c) « est un sous-groupe de G »** (§ structure de G). This is on the page (p. 29), but nothing argues it. The abstract lemma of p. 31 only yields a set of representatives of G_0\G, and G(c) ≃ G/G_0 is a bijection of sets. Either justify it or footnote it as the page's unproved claim.
2. **Footnote on G/G_0:** « de dimension 3g-3 et non 4g-4 ». h^0(ω^{⊗3}) = 5g-5 for g ≥ 2 (4g-4 is deg ω^2). Correct the number.
3. **Theorem of page 25:** the extension is written 0 → Ω → E → O_S → 0, where the reading's own convention (page 8 footnote) requires O_X. The direction is also u(C) = ĉ, while the quoted p. 26 has u(ĉ) = C. Harmonise, and footnote the page's O_S.
4. **Conn\*:** it is defined twice differently. In item c) of p. 26 it means "regular connections whose image is of the form ĉ"; in § L'énoncé final it means "les connexions régulières". On p. 33 the page defines Conn\*(Ŝ) as the regular connections and Conn\*(P) as its inverse image. Use that.
5. **ϖ_i « donc une section globale »:** these components are defined in a local model P ≃ Ŝym-hat(Ω), so they are local sections. Say « une section (sur l'ouvert où le modèle est choisi) ».
6. **Page 10 matrix:** the matrix (0 ±id; id φ_0) of an alternating form must be antisymmetric, (0 ε·id; -ε·id φ_0). The page has ±. Footnote it or correct it.
7. **Quotation, p. 8:** « si elle [provient] d'une ∞-connexion de P = P(E), laquelle sera dès lors déterminée ». The transcription reads « si elle \ill{} pour une ∞-connexion \ill{} de P = P(E) (…), laquelle sera dès lors \ill{} déterminée ». So « d'une » replaces « pour une », and the elisions are unmarked.
8. **Quotation, p. 7:** « d'un cocycle » is struck on the page. The footnote cites it among the words that « sont là » without saying so.
9. **Quotation, p. 16:** « l'espace analytique associé à une solution sur C, lisse de dimension 6g-6 » silently omits « (de \ill{}) » and a struck « mais ».
10. **Quotation, p. 31:** « alors hc est de la forme h·c′ » drops « ∈ C ».
11. **Quotation, p. 12:** « Epi (car. ≠ 2) » drops « (car \ill{}) ». Quotations at pp. 15 and 20 expand the page's abbreviations (« pt », « dim », « 2 inv. ») inside guillemets. Keep them, or bracket them.
12. **Bracketed fill-ins in quotations:** in « on suppose que l'image [est] constructible [et] algébrique » (p. 17) and « tel [qu'il prolonge] u_2 » (p. 26), the brackets stand where the transcription has « par \ill{} » and a struck phrase. Mark them as conjectural, or use [...].
13. **Page 18:**
    - The page writes c ∈ H^1(X, O). The reading writes Ext^1(O_X, ω) = H^1(X, ω), which is right, but there is no footnote.
    - The argument "u acts on ω by λ, hence multiplies c by λ" needs the normalisation u = id on E/ω ≅ O_X (automorphisms of P(E) are taken modulo scalars). State it.
14. **Page 21, « (de car. nulle ?) »:** the question mark is dropped. Footnote that the page asks.
    - In the editorial fix for A_4, S_4, A_5, the étale cover has degree |ρ(π)| (the order of the monodromy image), not |Γ|.
15. **Page 22 paragraph:** « un sous-fibré en droites invariant aurait le degré g-1, incompatible avec … Harder-Narasimhan » is muddled. In the standard argument an invariant line subbundle is flat, hence of degree 0, and maps non-trivially to the quotient ω^{-1/2}, of degree 1-g < 0. The Résumé's « un sous-fibré invariant aurait le degré g-1 » has the same problem: in the page's argument it is the quotient L that has degree g-1.
16. **Literature, Résumé and page 12:**
    - The quadric count (1/2)(g-2)(g-3) = dim I_2 holds for non-hyperelliptic X. The Résumé states it without that condition; the body has it.
    - The final footnote's « la paramétrisation de Gunning … (« Projective structures and projective bundles over compact Riemann surfaces », arXiv:0706.3608) » reads as if that paper were Gunning's. It is by F. Loray and D. Marín (arXiv, 2007). Name the authors.
17. **Prose:**
    - The section title « Ce que le dossier cherche, et ce qui lui manque » is a « Ce que le … » turn. Suggested: « L'intention du dossier, et ses manques ».
    - « ce que cette note ajoute est le troisième cas » (footnote, p. 21) is a pseudo-cleft. Suggested: « cette note ajoute le troisième cas ».
    - The gloss on page 19, « les coefficients −2 et 0 étant ceux de l'action adjointe sur les vecteurs de poids extrême », is not supported by the page (there are three values, −2, 1, 0) and explains nothing. Drop it or justify it.
