## Major issues

### M1. Page 4: « tout fibré vectoriel est somme directe d'un fibré trivial et de son déterminant » is false on a projective curve

- **Location:** §« Le même énoncé en K-théorie », « Le côté contravariant », the sentence after cl(V) = Σ(rg_i(V) − 1)·1_i + cl(det V), and its footnote (« C'est le théorème de structure des fibrés sur un schéma de dimension ≤ 1 à faisceau ample, et c'est là que sert l'hypothèse d'amplitude »).
- **Observation:** On P^1, O(1) ⊕ O(1) is not isomorphic to O ⊕ O(2). More generally, vector bundles on a projective curve do not split off trivial summands. Serre's splitting theorem is an affine statement. What holds is the identity *in K^0*:
  - with an ample line bundle, every vector bundle has a filtration with line-bundle quotients, so cl(V) = Σ cl(L_j);
  - since I(X)^2 = 0, Σ(cl(L_j) − 1) = cl(det V) − 1.
- **Evidence:** batch-01, p. 4 (lines 221-231). The page only writes the K-theoretic identity cl(E) = [rg(E), c^1(E)] with c^1(E) = det E. It does not claim any direct-sum decomposition. The false reason, and the role it gives the ampleness hypothesis, are the reading's own.
- **Why it matters:** The reading is held to being correct as it stands, and the footnote misattributes the role of the ample hypothesis.
- **Proposed fix:** Replace the sentence with the filtration plus I^2 = 0 argument. In the footnote, say that ampleness gives the line-bundle filtration (V(n) generated by global sections), and that the identity holds in K^0, not as an isomorphism of bundles.

### M2. Page 2: the Proposition and « E = Φ + D » are false in the free group, which is how the reading tells the reader to take E

- **Location:** §« L'anneau des classes ».
  - The body says « Le produit tensoriel en fait un anneau, que le feuillet note E. C'est … K_0(X) ».
  - The footnote to N then says « pourvu qu'on prenne E pour le groupe libre et E/N pour l'anneau des classes ».
  - Then come the starred lines E = Φ + D, Φ ∩ D = 0, and the « Proposition (page 2) » that N is generated by the two families.
- **Observation:** In the free group on isomorphism classes of coherent sheaves, the following fail:
  - E = Φ + D fails. The basis element [O_X ⊕ k_x], or [O_{2x}], is neither in the subgroup generated by locally free sheaves nor in the one generated by skyscrapers.
  - The Proposition fails. The relation [k_x ⊕ k_x] − 2[k_x] lies in N, yet the basis element k_x ⊕ k_x occurs in none of the proposed generators: these involve only vector bundles and the quotients O(V)/f·O(W) ≅ O_D. So N is strictly larger than the subgroup they generate.
  - In the ring E/N, on the other hand, E = Φ + D is not a direct-sum statement either. Classes of bundles already span, so Φ ∩ D ≠ 0 there, since [k_x] = [O] − [O(−x)].
  - The body also oscillates between the two readings of E (quotient ring, then free group).
- **Evidence:** batch-01, p. 2, lines 89-140. The page itself defines E as the quotient (« E anneau des classes … mod. les (F)−(F′)−(F″) ») and then N « sous-groupe de E engendré par » the same elements, so the page is self-contradictory. The reading's footnote resolves this in a way that makes the starred claims false.
- **Why it matters:** These are the first statements of the folder. A reader following the footnote's convention gets false assertions without warning.
- **Proposed fix:** State a true version. For instance:
  - « Soit E′ ⊂ E le sous-groupe engendré par les fibrés et les faisceaux O_D ; dans E′, E′ = Φ + D, Φ ∩ D = 0, et N ∩ E′ est engendré par les deux familles », with the general statement for E/N;
  - or present the Proposition as a presentation of K_0(X) = E/N by bundles and divisors.

  Footnote that, taken literally in the free group of all coherent sheaves, the page's starred claims fail (give the k_x ⊕ k_x example), and make the body consistent with whichever convention is chosen.

## Minor issues

1. **Sign, p. 13 paragraph:** « le calcul exact donne c(i_!x) = 1 + (−1)^{r−1}·r!·x′·η^r lorsque x′^2 = 0 ». The correct expression is 1 − (−1)^{r−1}·r!·x′·η^r. This follows from Q^{(r)}_1 = −r·c^1, or from the reading's own ch-formula, and the later footnote on the N.B. has the right sign. The conclusion is unaffected.
2. **Last page:** « c^2(O_C) = ½[C^2 − C·k_Y + f_*(k_{C̃})] ». The computation gives c^2(f_*O_{C̃}), which equals c^2(O_C) only when C is smooth. For singular C, f_*O_{C̃} = O_C + (length-δ skyscrapers), and c^2 shifts by −δ, giving p_a = g + δ. The closing footnote (« c'est la version de gauche qui reste vraie ») is too vague. Write c^2(f_*O_{C̃}) and state the singular-case correction.
3. **Résumé:** « y répondre deux lignes plus bas, « Non » ». On p. 21, « Non » follows the question on the same line (batch-02, line 177).
4. **Section heading « Classique pour les Courbes »:** the transcription marks this title as uncertain (`\section*{\uncertain{Classique pour les Courbes}}`, batch-01 line 79). The reading quotes it three times as certain. Footnote it once.
5. **Quotation, p. 13 N.B.:** « les coefficients de la classe de Chern sont nuls ; sans qu'elle soit nulle ». The transcription has « les coefficients \ill{} deux \ill{} \ill{} de la classe de Chern sont nuls ; sans que elle soit nulle ». The illegible words are dropped without […], and « que elle » is normalised.
6. **Lean paths stale:** the footnotes cite `lean/Grothendieck/AlgebraicGeometry/Folder2.lean`. That directory no longer exists in the project (`lean/` holds Bourbaki and Quillen only). The file is `GrothendieckArchivesProofs/Grothendieck/AlgebraicGeometry/Folder2.lean`, and the header comment uses the old path too. Update after the #160 migration.
7. **« l'unique morphisme de modules K^• → K_• est x ↦ x·[O_X] »:** module maps are x ↦ x·m for any m. Say « le morphisme de modules qui envoie 1 sur [O_X] ».
8. **p. 9 paragraph:** « où Q^{(r)}_{i−r} est, par définition, la partie linéaire … » mixes the page's index (i−r) with the reading's (i). Align it with the display just above.
9. **Prop. 3 (p. 21):** that CB is a polynomial ring (hence torsion-free) is asserted. The page says only « CB est sans torsion ». Say « (ce que la page affirme) », or justify it.
10. **Pages 15-16:**
    - « u^*u_*(F) ≃ ⊔_{G′/G} F … G permutant les facteurs par translation » is imprecise as a G-module statement (Mackey). Keep the O-Module statement only.
    - The letter G is used both for a group and for a module in the same sentence (« H un second groupe, G ∈ A(X, H) »). Rename the module.
11. **p. 36 footnote, « ce que le théorème de Riemann-Roch singulier fera vingt ans plus tard »:** Baum-Fulton-MacPherson is 1975, about 9-18 years after the plausible dates of the page. Write « dans les années 1970 ».
12. **Résumé:** « Quand Grothendieck, quelques années plus tard, cherchera un « Riemann-Roch sans dénominateurs » ». Unsourced as to who posed it and when (it is in SGA 6, and proved by Jouanolou in 1970). Add the reference or soften it.
