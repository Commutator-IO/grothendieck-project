## Batch 09

Pages looked at: 171, 173.

- p. 171, hypothesis: « $F$ soit plat `\uncertain{$=$}` $G$ : fibres injectives » → « plat $=$ $G$ : … », flag dropped, `\note{}` added. The sign is a clear double stroke at the end of the line; it links the two hypotheses (F flat; G with injective stalks), which is what the proof uses (« supposons d'abord G : fibres injectives », flat generators i_!(A_{X′})). It is not « F = G ».
- p. 171, (ii), first relation: « Ext^i(·, G) = 0 pour i > n » → « pour i > 2n ». **Touches the statement.** On the 1000 dpi crop the bound, written above the line, has a cursive « 2 » joined to the « n », drawn more loosely than the « 2n » of the line below; « i > n » is excluded by the ink and would be false for H^i(X, G) (H^2(P^1, A) ≠ 0). The first argument is an A written over an X (or the reverse), i.e. the relation is H^i(X, G) = Ext^i(A_X, G) = 0 for i > 2n (SGA 4 X 4.3). The note was rewritten accordingly.
- p. 173: « on peut supposer $G$ `\ill{}` constructible » → « `\uncertain{injectif}` constructible », with a `\note{}`: the word begins with a dotted i and a looped j; its ending is drawn out (« injectif » or « injective »). It agrees with the bracketed question that follows (G as a colimit of constructible sheaves with injective stalks).
- The rest of p. 173 (the bracket, « filtrante », the « ? » in the margin) reads as transcribed.
