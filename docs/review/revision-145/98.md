The letter is an « o »; the arrows marked « can » in the same diagrams have a clear « a ». The note on p. 6 of the batch (« Can », written « Cm » there, « settled by page 10 ») and `98.modern.tex` are to re-check; p. 6 was not looked at in this pass.
