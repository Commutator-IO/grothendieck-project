# Issue 145 (B), folder 151: the pages that statements lean on

Pass of 14 November 2026, Fable 5.2 (`claude-fable-5-2`). **A machine pass, not checked by a reader.** Facsimile: the Montpellier PDF (PDF page = archive page + 1), rendered at 300 dpi in full-width strips, the doubtful places on crops at 400 to 800 dpi (the scans are about 250 dpi natively).

Variant B of the pass: only the pages a statement leans on. "Blind" pages (cited by the Lean article through `\lot`) were transcribed afresh before the batch file was opened, then diffed against it, each disagreement settled on a crop. "Targeted" pages (cited by a finding only) were looked at where they carry `\uncertain` or `\ill`. No other page of the folder was touched.

Counts are of macros in the text of the pages concerned. "Read" is an `\ill` replaced by words; "confirmed" and "corrected" are `\uncertain` whose flag was dropped, with the word kept or changed. A gap replaced by a reading that is still doubtful is flagged `\uncertain` and counted as left.

**Not done: batch 04** (blind p. 62; targeted pp. 63–68 and 74, about 50 flags). The pass was stopped at the batch boundary on the editor's budget cap.
