Pass of 14 November 2026, Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`). **A machine pass, not checked by a reader.** Variant B: only the pages that a statement leans on. Pages the Lean article cites (`\lot` in `docs/lean/sections/161-6.tex`) were transcribed afresh from the facsimile before the batch file was opened, then diffed against it word by word, each disagreement settled on a crop; on those pages and on the pages a finding cites, every `\uncertain` and `\ill` was looked at. Counts are of macros (`\uncertain`, `\ill`), as in the issue. "Read" is an `\ill` replaced by words; "confirmed" and "corrected" are `\uncertain` whose flag was dropped, with the word kept or changed. A gap or a doubtful word replaced by another doubtful reading is counted as left.
