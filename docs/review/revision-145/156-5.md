Counts are of macros (`\uncertain`, `\ill`) on the pages looked at. "Read" is an `\ill` replaced by words; "confirmed" and "corrected" are `\uncertain` whose flag was dropped, with the word kept or changed; "removed" is a gap where the line carries no word. A gap read only from the outline of the word and the sentence is now an `\uncertain` reading and counted as left. Where a split is marked "about", the totals before and after are exact and the split is tallied from the edits.
