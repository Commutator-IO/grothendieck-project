**Consequences, not applied.**

- **Lean card, `\lot{153}{1}{3}`**: the card states the lemma with « F ∈ K_0[T] ⊗_{k_0} M »; the page has « K_0[T] ⊗_{K_0} M_{K_0} ». If M_{K_0} is M ⊗_{k_0} K_0 the two modules are canonically the same, so the mathematics of the card stands; its wording of the page does not.
- **Finding `153-relative-int-coproducts`**: its claim and basis quote « K_0[X,Y] = K_0[X] ⊗_{k_0} K_0[Y] » and build the edition's extra hypothesis K_0 ⊗_{k_0} K_0 = K_0 on that subscript. If the page writes ⊗_{K_0} (now flagged), the identity is a triviality and the hypothesis moves elsewhere: to the step from F(X+Y) ∈ K_0[X] ⊗_{K_0} K_0[Y] to an element of Ω ⊗_{k_0} Ω, which is what the lemma (with M = Ω, free) is for. To be re-examined once question 2 is settled.
