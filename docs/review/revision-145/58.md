On the 600 dpi crop the word after « sur X' » is the same word as the struck « sont » three words earlier (same ductus, not struck); there is no second « G' » on the line. **This contradicts the earlier revision of 18 October 2026**, on which the finding's basis quotes « G′ de prof ≥ 2 en les pts fermés de X′ − Y′ ». As written, the depth hypothesis bears on F' and G' both; the reduction two lines below (H = Hom(F, G), « si F est de prof ≥ 2 en les pts [fermés] de X' − Y ») uses only that of G'. **Consequences, not applied**: the finding's `basis` should quote the page as « F′, G′ coh sur X′ sont de prof ≥ 2 en les pts fermés x de X′ − Y′ » and say that restricting the hypothesis to G is the reading's (it is what the reduction through Hom(F, G) uses); the Lean card's « dès que G est de profondeur ≥ 2 » is then a strengthening by the reading of what the page writes, not the page's wording; the modernised reading should be checked at the same place. The point of the finding (nothing asked at the points of Y') is not touched. A note in the text says this.
- Also there: « pts » stands before the struck « x ∈ Φ … », and an « x » follows « fermés » (added).
