# Issue 145, folder 156-9: revision on the facsimile

Pass: Fable 5.2 (`claude-fable-5-2`), 14 November 2026. Every reading below is a machine pass on the Montpellier facsimile (rendered at 300 dpi, in colour, read on strips of a third of a page and on enlarged crops), **not checked by a reader**. The modernised reading (`156-9.modern.tex`), the findings and the Lean statements are not edited by this pass; what leans on a changed reading is listed for `/modernize-grothendieck` and `/find-novelty`.

The hand of this folder is very fast (most letters are a wave), so many words are settled by the sentence and the mathematics rather than by the letters. The counts of confirmed, corrected and read places are approximate (to about ten): they were tallied from the edits, not place by place.
