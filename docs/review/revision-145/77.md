Variant B of the pass: only the pages that a statement leans on. Pages the Lean article cites (`\lot` in `docs/lean/sections/77.tex`, and p. 132) are read blind, then diffed against the existing text; pages a finding cites (`src/content/findings.ts`) and the Lean article does not get the targeted check of every `\uncertain` and `\ill`. Other pages of a batch are not touched.

Counts are of macros (`\uncertain`, `\ill`) on the pages looked at. "Read" is an `\ill` replaced by words; "confirmed" and "corrected" are `\uncertain` whose flag was dropped, with the word kept or changed. A gap or a doubtful word replaced by another doubtful reading is counted as left.
