# Issue 128 — survey of figures described in prose with no `\drawing` mark (2026-11-10)

Read-only survey of every transcribed folder under `transcripts/` except 156-2, 156-3, 156-4, 156-5, 156-6, 156-7, 81 and 161-6. **Judged from the transcription text only: no facsimile was opened.** Nothing in the repository was edited.

Method. In each `batch-NN.fr.tex` every `\note{…}`, `\marginal{…}` and `%` comment not inside a `\drawing{…}` was searched for: dessin, croquis, esquisse, figure, sketch, drawn, drawing, doodle, griffonn-, gribouill-, scribbl-, picture, petit(s) cercle(s) (first pass, 646 units read); schéma, diagramme, flèches together with « non redessiné / pas reproduit / décrit » (second pass, 24 units); and a third pass on notes that name a shape with an article (« un cercle », « deux sphères », « un tétraèdre », « un segment »…) without any of the words above (301 units). Units with a `\drawing` mark within three lines were set aside as covered; for the others the marks and tikz figures of the same page were listed and compared with the note.

Classes. **d** — a real figure described in prose, no mark for it. **struck/doodle** — a struck-out sketch or a decorative scribble (scribbles that merely cancel a formula or a word are deletions, not figures, and are not listed). **t** (additional list, not asked for as such) — an arrow diagram or other relation that could be set as text (array, tikz-cd) but is only described in a note: no `\drawing` mark is due; it belongs to the transcription skill.

Counting. One row is one note (a note may describe several sketches). Review lines are one per page.
