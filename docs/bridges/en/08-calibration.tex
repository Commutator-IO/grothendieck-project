One output deserves a word, because it reads well for engineers even though the mathematics is known. For an abstract rewriting system $(S,\to)$, give $S$ the topology whose open sets are the sets of terms closed under rewriting. Then \emph{the system is confluent (Church--Rosser) iff this space is extremally disconnected}: this is the third line of the table applied to the preorder $\to^*$. Confluence of $\beta$-reduction thus says that the space of $\lambda$-terms, with the sets closed under $\beta$-reduction as open sets, is extremally disconnected. We did not find this reading in the rewriting literature, but since it is a direct restatement of a published theorem we do not count it as new.
