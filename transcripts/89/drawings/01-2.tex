A freehand closed curve, wider than high and flattened at the top and lower left; on it, alternately, four full points and four crosses, at the places measured on the page; the arc from the upper point round the left to the lower left point, which passes through one cross, is gone over heavily, and a second heavy stroke runs straight from the upper point towards that cross. It is the picture of the duality between vertices and sides of a polygon (S and A) of which the page speaks. The note of the transcription says « un arc épaissi entre deux points »: the heavy arc runs between two consecutive points and carries the cross between them. The cross on the heavy arc is larger and has an extra stroke on the page. Nothing struck.
