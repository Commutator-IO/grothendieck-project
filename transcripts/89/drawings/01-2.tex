A freehand circle drawn as a circle; on it, alternately, four full points and four crosses, at the angles read on the page (computed points of the circle); the arc from the upper point round the left to the lower left point, which passes through one cross, is gone over heavily. It is the picture of the duality between vertices and sides of a polygon (S and A) of which the page speaks. The note of the transcription says « un arc épaissi entre deux points »: the heavy arc runs between two consecutive points and carries the cross between them. The cross on the heavy arc is larger and has an extra stroke on the page. Nothing struck. Redrawn 2026-11-12 (issue 128): to check again.
