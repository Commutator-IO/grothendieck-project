The upper part of a circle, cut off by the lower edge of the sheet (which is itself cut obliquely), drawn as an arc of a circle fitted to his outline; a chord between two points of the circle, drawn several times on the page and once here; from its left end a stroke down to the edge and a thin broken stroke by the outline; from its right end a stroke down to the left as far as the edge; a small point on the circle at the right. Every chord ends on the computed points of the circle. The writing that crosses the circle (the last formulas and « deux faces deviennent 2 » with its vertical stroke) belongs to the text and is not drawn. Still differs: the pencil doublings of the outline and of the chord (three or four strokes each) are reduced to one; the faint strokes near the outline at the right are left out.
