# Typing conventions of the Bourbaki archive — what the passes have learned

Each pass adds a dated heading naming the rédaction, with what the next pass
should not have to work out again. Like the fonds' `hand.md`, this narrows a
reading and never settles one.

## 2026-10-10 — rédaction n° 433 (Fonds Pierre Cartier, PCR 119), Opus 5.5
- Clean carbon typescript of 1965. Mathematical signs (∈, ∪, ⊂, arrows,
  exponents, ∏) are typed with a symbol ball or added by hand in the blanks.
  The copy shows no distinction, so they are set in the formula without
  apparatus.
- Underlined capitals stand for named objects: `P` is the power set, typed
  `\underline{P}`; `Q` and `N` are kept underlined as typed.
- Quotation marks are typed " … ". They are rendered « … » in the French text.
- Archive pencil: cote « PCR 119 » top left, page number top right (= PDF
  page). The typist's « - 2 - » is the PDF page minus 1. Neither is transcribed.
