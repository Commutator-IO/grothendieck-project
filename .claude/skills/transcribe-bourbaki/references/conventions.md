# Typing conventions of the Bourbaki archive — what the passes have learned

Each pass adds a dated heading naming the rédaction, with what the next pass
should not have to work out again. Like the fonds' `hand.md`, this narrows a
reading and never settles one.

## 2026-10-10 — rédaction n° 433 (Fonds Pierre Cartier, PCR 119), Opus 5.5
- Clean carbon typescript of 1965. Mathematical signs (∈, ∪, ⊂, arrows,
  exponents, ∏) are typed with a symbol ball or added by hand in the blanks.
  The copy shows no distinction, so they are set in the formula without
  apparatus.
- Underlined capitals stand for named objects: `P` is the power set, typed
  `\underline{P}`; `Q` and `N` are kept underlined as typed.
- Quotation marks are typed " … ". They are rendered « … » in the French text.
- Archive pencil: cote « PCR 119 » top left, page number top right (= PDF
  page). The typist's « - 2 - » is the PDF page minus 1. Neither is transcribed.

## 2026-10-18 — rédactions 383 bis, 443, 455, 307, 327, 222, 417, 346, 288, 243, 245 and item 999 (Grothendieck), Opus 5.5
- Two copy sources, two paginations. Fonds Cartier (PCR) files open with the archive's cover sheet
  (PDF page 1, skipped; typist's « - n - » = PDF page minus 1). ENS-library and Delsarte (IECL)
  files (455, 222, 243, 245, 288) have no cover sheet: PDF page = typist's page, or the summary
  sheet comes first (243, 288, 346 have an unnumbered summary page).
- ENS copies carry a diagonal ink mention top left (« Archives Grothendieck - sept. 1965 », « Juillet
  1955 Grothendieck »…): an archival hand, set as \marginal + note « main non identifiée ». The
  archive's notice may misread its month (455: notice « octobre », page « sept. »).
- Cartier copies may carry « PC » / « P.C. » initials top right; his annotations are likely but not
  proven to be his: keep « main non identifiée ». 417 is heavily annotated in that hand (formula
  corrections, marginal programme notes, two annotated versos): budget 20 pages per batch for it.
- Hand-drawn letters in typed blanks: script L, F, T, C, E (\mathcal), gothic S, F, U, V, Is, M, p
  and the gothic R of the radical (\mathfrak). In 245 the gothic p of prime ideals looks like a « y »;
  in 243/245 the « Is » of isomorphism sheaves is drawn gothic on some pages and typed on others —
  follow the page. These are set in the formula without apparatus, like the 433 symbols.
- Exponents set above a letter for inverse/composed entourages (288) are \overset{-1}{V},
  \overset{2}{V}; underlined W of the function-space entourages kept \underline{W}; underlined
  ideals (327) \underline{J}, \underline{T}; underlined Aut of sheaves (243) \underline{\mathrm{Aut}}.
- Statements are underlined word by word, the symbols left out: \emph{} around the words only,
  math outside \emph. An \emph{} cannot cross a \page{} or a paragraph: close it and reopen.
- Typed interlinear corrections (a word or syllable typed above the line with a caret) are running
  text; only ink is apparatus.
- Bibliographic blanks « (réf) », « prop. » with no number, « (   ) » are kept as typed.

## 2026-10-23 — rédaction 270 (ENS copy, item 782), Opus 5.5
- No cover sheet; PDF page = typist's « - n - ». ENS diagonal ink mention p. 1 (« Archives Grothendieck. Juin 57 »).
- Fill-in hand draws a lowercase script k for the base ring (set $k$, as the typist also types plain k in
  the same role), Omega, wedge, tensor, theta, delta, and two gothic capitals: the Lie algebra of
  derivations (looks like a « Y » with a tail, read \mathfrak{G}) and a second one from p. 20
  (\mathfrak{A}; a gothic B joins it on p. 43, which settles the reading). KaTeX has no lowercase \mathscr: do not try it.
- Show-through from the verso (vertical strokes, pp. 29, 34) is not a reader's mark.
- § 4 (p. 134 on) restarts the formula numbers at (1), and the typist reuses numbers inside § 3
  ((57) twice, pp. 114 and 120; (38) twice, pp. 163-164): keep them as typed, note once.
- In the sheaf part the fill-in hand draws a script O for the structure sheaf (\mathscr{O}), a cursive
  « m » for maximal ideals (\mathfrak{m}), a gothic J of augmentation ideals like a « y » with a tail
  (\mathfrak{J}), the radical as a curled K (\mathfrak{R}), and a double-barred Q (\mathbb{Q}, noted; a
  typed plain Q beside it stays Q). Typed underlined « Hom » is the sheaf Hom, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}.
- Blanks the fill-in hand forgot (an arrow, a ≥, a § number) are kept empty with a \note, not supplied.

## 2026-10-23 — rédaction 326 bis (ENS copy, item 1008, EGA I typescript, 437 pp.), Opus 5.5
- A composite file: the typescript of chs. 0-III with the sheets of additions and corrections bound in
  where they were received (English instructions « P. 31, before prop. 7, add : », French text). Each
  sheet has its own numbering (roman, « II-i », « 0-i », « III-i »), and the chapters restart theirs
  (ch. 0 roman, ch. I arabic, then « II-n », « III-n »). The offset PDF − typist changes many times
  (−17, −20, −35, −36, −189, −200, −338, −328): give it per batch in the header. Duplicate scans
  (129/130, 175-176 = 179-180, 333-335 = 336-338) are transcribed once; pages the copy lacks
  (typist's 29, III-11 to III-20, the end of II-75) are noted, not supplied. Inserted « bis/ter » pages
  and an old and a new version of the same page are both kept.
- At least three typewriters: the first has a symbol ball (ε for ∈ → \in, circled x → \otimes); the later
  ones have none and a fill-in hand draws Greek letters, Γ, Φ, arrows, ∈, ⊂ in the blanks (no apparatus).
  Forgotten blanks stay empty with a \note.
- A small grey cursive, apparently on the stencil, makes real mathematical corrections in all chapters
  (insertions, wavy strikes, marginal references). Set \add/\struck/\marginal, « main non identifiée »:
  no initials or signature anywhere. Typed interlinear insertions (caret or arc) are running text —
  check each at 300 dpi.
- Typed letters kept as typed: ideals plain (a, p, j_x) in the first typescript, underlined later
  (\underline{p}, \underline{j}_y); underlined sheaves/algebras \underline{S}, \underline{A}(X); the
  barred ψ, φ of a morphism of ringed spaces \underline{\psi}; ᵃφ is {}^{a}\varphi; a typed tilde
  \widetilde{}, after a parenthesis (…)^{\sim}; « lim » with arrow \varinjlim / \varprojlim. The residue
  field follows the page: \kappa where a kappa stands beside a typed base field k, k(x) where typed k.
- Practicalities: braces round a tikz-cd label containing a comma; no \hfill or \qquad in prose; an
  underlined letter in prose goes inside $…$; a long struck passage is \struck{\ill{}} with any guess in
  a \note (a long struck \uncertain overflows).

## 2026-10-27 — rédactions 377 (item 933) and 551 (item 1102), attribution pass on 383, 384, 384 brouillon, 387, 391, 392, 397, 413, Opus 5.5
- 377 (Fonds Cartier, PCR 070): cover sheet, then typist's « - n - » = PDF page minus 1. Vectors typed with a
  double underline (\underline{\underline{v}}); underlined K(X) for compactly supported continuous functions; the
  second measure is a special sign read \nu, the product measure a « ϖ » read \pi. Annotated in ink by the
  owner's hand (« PC » top right of p. 2), plus a loose handwritten sheet « Lemme (Bruhat) » at the end.
- 551 (Fonds Boutet de Monvel, BDMR 011): the scan is faint; pdftoppm at 130 dpi is unreadable, so extract the
  600 dpi JPEGs with `pdfimages -j` and read half-pages at 1000 px wide with `-level 30%,88%`. Contents paged in
  roman (pp. 2-3); from p. 4 the typist's page = PDF page minus 3. Typewriter script letters for the Banach types
  (\mathscr{C}, \mathscr{L}, \mathscr{H}) and for the derived classes α with a script index; class symbols
  ε, π (typed like « ¶ »), η, θ. Diagonal ink « Archives P. Cartier » on pp. 2 and 26 (archival).
- Attribution pass (Fonds Cartier copies, PCR 075-101): every annotated page seen carries one cursive ink hand,
  the hand of the « PC » / « P.C. » initials on the title pages (383, 384, 392, 413). No page shows
  Grothendieck's hand; nothing in them is his to transcribe. Keep « main non identifiée » if any is ever
  transcribed, since the initials make Cartier likely but do not prove the marginalia are his.
